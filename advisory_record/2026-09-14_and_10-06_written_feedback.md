# Advisor's written feedback on the drafts, 14 September and 6 October 2026

*Record kept by the author. The 14 September comment is quoted; the 6 October comments are the author's summary of the advisor's written notes. The handwritten comments of 11 and 20 October are transcribed in separate files.*

## 14 September 2026 — comments on the draft of Sections 1–3 (sent 9 September)

> "The Introduction could be more concise, and make the key points clearer. Also, try to use plain academic language throughout the paper."

Response: the introduction was shortened and its findings paragraph rewritten to list the main findings in order; figurative phrasing was removed from Sections 1 and 2, and the same plain style was applied to all later sections.

## 6 October 2026 — meeting and written comments on the full draft

The advisor found the content and results interesting and asked for the following:

1. The paper uses too many uncommon terms and technical details; it should be readable by a master's student, not only by an econometrician.
2. The introduction should summarise the regressions in plain words before the results and list the contributions clearly.
3. The introduction should explain why the paper studies exposed prefectures rather than the regulated cities.
4. The choice of variables and functional forms (for example the quadratic controls) should be motivated.
5. The challenges to identification should be discussed more clearly, one by one.
6. Are renters and people who move included in the sample? Results should be robust to them.

Response: terms were defined or replaced throughout and method detail moved to Section 5.5 and the appendices (1); the introduction now describes the regression and numbers the contributions (2) and explains the exposed-prefecture design (3); Section 4 motivates the controls (4) and lists the challenges to identification (5); Section 3.3 states that renters and movers are included, and Table 8 adds owners-only and drop-movers estimates (6).
