UNIFICATION (precise): the shock redistributes the fertility decision
to the wife and so SUPPRESSES births where she wants fewer than the
husband (Fact 2, causal, total kids, p=0.02). The wife is the 'fewer'
party on SONS specifically in daughter-first one-child families (Fact 1,
p=0.003). => the son-chasing 2nd birth of daughter-first families is
suppressed = the locked headline. One mechanism, two preference
dimensions. Seam: son-disagreement is 2018-only (qka203), so the SON
causal suppression itself can't be pre/post-estimated.
